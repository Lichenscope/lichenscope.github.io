Current positions

Jan 2026 –

Founder and Independent Researcher — LichenScope, France

I design and lead independent biodiversity research projects, from writing proposals and preparing budgets to coordinating externally funded work. My expeditions in the Caribbean generate specimen-based datasets for taxonomic and evolutionary studies. I am also preparing fieldwork in French Guiana and have developed DNA barcoding workflows to support biodiversity assessments.

Scientific Collaborator (unpaid appointment) — Herbarium BR, Meise Botanic Garden, Belgium

I study tropical lichen collections at BR, focusing on new species descriptions, taxonomic revisions and collection documentation.

Sep 2025 –

Scientific Collaborator (unpaid appointment) — BEC laboratory, University of Liège, Belgium

I study phylogenetic relationships and diversification in the lichen-forming fungal family Gomphillaceae.

Previous research experience

2026

Data Analyst / Research Officer (3 months) — Botanical Society of Liège, Belgium

For MycoWeb, a project on macrofungi in Wallonia, I curated and validated fungal biodiversity datasets and contributed to Red List pre-assessments.

2025

Research Officer (2 months) — Herbarium PC, National Museum of Natural History (MNHN), Paris, France

2021–2025

PhD Researcher (FNRS–FRIA Fellow) — University of Liège, Belgium

I led a global multilocus phylogenetic study of Gomphillaceae based on 1,256 specimens, revealing extensive hidden diversity and regional endemism (IMA Fungus, 2025).

2019–2021

Research Assistant — Herbarium P, MNHN, Paris, France

For FEnTOM (Endemic Flora of the French Overseas Territories), I curated, standardised and integrated taxonomic, occurrence and herbarium data for more than 30,000 specimens of endemic plants from 13 French overseas territories. These data supported interoperable biodiversity analyses and national Red List pre-assessments.
